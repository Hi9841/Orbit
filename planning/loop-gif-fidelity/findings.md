# Findings

- Downloaded GIF and pinned upstream `assets/graphics/loop_demo.gif` have the same SHA-256: `2B23D1C46655C2FD21F5491601415A2EED63C6EB2C04C278F0F56AA91A90F3FF`. It is 1267 x 713, 239 frames, 10 seconds. Review frames are in `.tools/loop-demo-review/`.
- The demo surfaces are dark, blurred, rounded, and lightly outlined. The radial appears as a roughly 60 px rounded square at GIF scale, with a gray body and an off-white selected arc. The large blurred rectangles are demonstration windows; Orbit should style its own preview, not alter the target app windows.
- Pinned `PreviewView.swift` layers macOS `.hudWindow` blur, a 10% accent gradient, a 1 pt quinary edge, and a configured accent stroke. Pinned `RadialMenuView.swift` uses HUD material inside a ring and a light thin border. Source paths: `.tools/upstream-loop/Loop-df26d565e07c82e156b8f1c361bdcf428f32e3a4/Loop/Window Action Indicators/`.
- Orbit currently has a flat `HUD_COLOR` bitmap with global opacity, a saved 8 px accent border and 22 px corner radius, and a circular radial (`radial_corner_radius=50`) with cyan cap. That explains the visible mismatch.
- Microsoft documents `DWMSBT_TRANSIENTWINDOW` as Desktop Acrylic behind the full window bounds on Windows 11 build 22621+: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/dwmapi/ne-dwmapi-dwm_systembackdrop_type . This machine reports build 26200. Compatibility with Orbit's layered color-key popup remains to be measured.
- The disposable `acrylic_probe` returned `Ok(())` for `DwmSetWindowAttribute` on normal, layered color-key, and layered alpha popups. The desktop screenshot `.tools/acrylic-probe.png` showed the first two as opaque flat gray and the third as translucent flat gray. None blurred what was behind them. The return code alone was insufficient.
- The fallback captures only the destination frame with GDI while Orbit's two popups are hidden, restores the radial immediately, blurs the pixels in memory, and composites a dark neutral plate above them. It saves no screenshot. The plate bitmap cache now includes its screen position and opacity and clears after a radial session or drag.
